Where Pets & People Connect

Enjoy delicious food, refreshing drinks, and heartwarming moments with your furry friends.

关于我们 | About Us

MiMiaoHe Café is a coffee shop that blends the nostalgic charm of classic Hong Kong-style diners with a pet-friendly environment. We warmly welcome all cat and owners to enjoy snacks and traditional Hong Kong beverages with their furry companions, embracing the warmth and hospitality reminiscent of old-school Nanyang cafés.


Our Cats

Meet Our Café Cats

Mi Mi
She’s the only girl here — her name is Mi Mi. She’s the princess of this café and can be a bit hard to deal with. A picky eater, shy at first, but once she gets to know you, she loves head rubs. Acting cute, begging for attention, and snuggling up to her owner — that’s her secret weapon.
Miao Miao
He is a boy named Miao Miao — the fattest one here. By the way, he's the café manager, in charge of every cat’s behavior. He's kind of shy around strangers. But here's the thing: if you have cat food in your hand, he could become your best and most intimate friend — at least until the food is gone.
He He
He’s a boy named HeHe, and his smiling face is his iconic trademark. He serves every customer with his best and is an optimistic, positive cat known for healing hearts. He loves to chat and is always meowing with his owner — a great companion in this café.
