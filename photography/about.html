About the Artist

I document fleeting moments — the kind that feel like they already belong to the past.

Every frame is a love letter to cities that never sleep and faces that tell stories words can't capture. Blending the nostalgia of analog film with the precision of modern technique, I create images that exist somewhere between memory and dream.

Based between Kuala Lumpur and the neon-soaked streets of East Asia, my work draws from the cinematic worlds of Wong Kar-Wai and the analog textures of 90s street photography. Each project is a collaboration — a shared vision brought to life through light, shadow, and honest emotion.
